本项目是非官方东方Project粉丝创作。角色与世界观来源于上海爱丽丝幻乐团 / ZUN；本项目不声称与官方合作，也不声称拥有东方角色IP。

## 生成主景的完整提示词

使用内置 `image_gen`，生成全新图片，非编辑第三方图像；原始输出已复制到项目art目录，构建时进入前端资源。未使用CLI/API密钥模式。

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: website hero background illustration, very wide landscape 16:9 composition. Create a refined original anime background painting of the Scarlet Devil Mansion from Touhou Project at a tranquil moonlit night: an elegant Victorian gothic manor of deep burgundy brick with pointed spires, warm amber lit arched stained-glass windows and a central clock tower; rose garden and wrought iron gate foreground, a distant lake and blue violet mist, a large pale ivory full moon behind the tower, fine silver stars, beautiful painterly clouds. Mansion placed in the RIGHT two thirds, LEFT third is dark atmospheric midnight indigo sky and faint tree silhouettes with ample unobstructed negative space for actual website title text. Restrained colors midnight #11101c, wine red #8b1e32, antique gold #d6b47a, dusty lavender #ac91c9. Rich detailed hand painted environmental concept art, delicate architecture, cinematic depth, peaceful and inviting, polished Japanese fantasy visual-novel background, elegant dark fantasy not horror. NO text, NO lettering, NO logos, NO interface, NO watermark, NO characters, NO blood, NO skulls. This is artwork only, not a webpage mockup.
```

生成图片约2.68MB，首屏只预先加载这一幅，其他封面与正文图片使用lazy loading；封面SVG通常低于2KB。后续可通过构建工具提供WebP/AVIF与srcset，当前未声称已输出这些格式。
